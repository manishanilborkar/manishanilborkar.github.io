01 / About

I build digital hardware from high-level models to working FPGA systems.

I am a Junior Research Fellow in the Department of Electronics Engineering at IIT Jodhpur, working on an ISRO-funded project on high-level synthesizable digital designs using C, C++, and Python.

My work sits at the intersection of hardware architecture, RTL design, FPGA implementation, and verification. I am especially interested in energy-efficient digital VLSI and hardware accelerators for edge computing.

02 / RF ACCELERATION

Digital Predistortion

HLS · AXI4-Stream · Vivado · Zybo Z7

C++ · Vitis HLS · Digital design
