Manish Anil Borkar

Project Archive

Manish is a Junior Research Fellow in the Department of Electronics Engineering, IIT Jodhpur. He is currently working on an ISRO-funded project titled “High-Level Synthesizable Digital Designs Modelled by using C/C++/Python.”

His work focuses on developing digital hardware from high-level C/C++ models to synthesizable RTL and working FPGA systems. His experience includes hardware architecture design, High-Level Synthesis, RTL integration, AXI interfaces, FPGA implementation and functional verification.

He is currently working on a DDR4 HLS controller, Digital Predistortion hardware and a JESD204B/C communication model. His work involves Vitis HLS, Vivado, SystemVerilog, Questa and FPGA boards such as the ZCU104, ZCU102 and Zybo Z7.

His research interests include Digital VLSI Design, FPGA-based systems, High-Level Synthesis, hardware accelerators and energy-efficient Edge AI hardware.

Before joining IIT Jodhpur, he completed his B.Tech in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering from Government College of Engineering, Amravati, under Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University.
