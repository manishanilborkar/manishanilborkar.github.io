I am a Junior Research Fellow in the Department of Electronics Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur . I am currently contributing to the ISRO-sponsored project “High-Level Synthesizable Digital Designs Modelled by using C/C++/Python.”

My work focuses on developing synthesizable digital hardware using C++, High-Level Synthesis and FPGA platforms. I work through the complete design flow, beginning with C simulation and C/RTL co-simulation and continuing through RTL generation, IP packaging, Vivado block-design integration, synthesis, implementation, timing analysis and hardware testing.

I am currently developing an HLS-based DDR4 controller scheduler that supports bank and row management, command scheduling, refresh handling, burst transfers, timing constraints and AXI-based interfaces. I am also developing a Digital Predistortion hardware module using memory-polynomial and LUT-based techniques with AXI4-Stream interfaces.

I have developed a JESD204B transport and link-layer model supporting multi-lane data mapping, K28.5 synchronization and 8b/10b encoding and decoding. I validate my hardware designs on ZCU102, ZCU104 and Zybo Z7 FPGA platforms and evaluate their latency, throughput, timing performance and resource utilization.

I completed my Bachelor of Technology in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering from Government College of Engineering, Amravati, under Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University in July 2023. I graduated with a CGPA of 7.37/10.

My research interests include Digital VLSI Design, FPGA and SoC Design, High-Level Synthesis, memory-system design, RTL verification, computer architecture and hardware acceleration.

HLS-Based DDR4 Controller

I am developing a DDR4 controller scheduler with bank and row management, command scheduling, refresh handling, burst transfers, timing constraints and AXI-based interfaces. I am also performing RTL verification and FPGA validation of the generated design.

Current research work
Digital Predistortion Hardware

I am developing a Digital Predistortion hardware module using memory-polynomial and LUT-based techniques. The design uses AXI4-Stream interfaces for transferring digital samples and is being prepared for FPGA implementation and hardware testing.

IIT Jodhpur research work
JESD204B Transport and Link-Layer Model

I developed a JESD204B transport and link-layer model supporting multi-lane data mapping, K28.5 synchronization and 8b/10b encoding and decoding.

2023
AXI4 Memory-Mapped Hardware IP Using Vitis HLS

I developed synthesizable C++ hardware modules using AXI4 Master and AXI4-Lite Slave interfaces. I generated the RTL using AMD Vitis HLS, packaged it as reusable hardware IP and integrated it into a Zynq-7000 Vivado block design.

2023
RTL Design and Verification of a Fibonacci Generator

I designed a hierarchical and synthesizable Verilog RTL architecture using register, arithmetic, control and output modules. I also developed a testbench and verified the sequence generation, reset operation and control behaviour using QuestaSim and Synopsys VCS.
